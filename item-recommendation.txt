1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Each Character / Draco Companion possesses 5 Equipment Slots (flexible across
  Weapons, Armor, Boots, Accessories, and Relics).
- Equipment is stored in a Shared Inventory Bag. Any unlocked character can equip
  any available equipment piece from the bag as long as there are unequipped
  copies in reserve.
- Each character maintains its own independent equipment loadout.
- Equipment stat enhancements are dynamically added to the character's base stats
  and applied in real-time during campaign combat and hero preview arenas.

2. EQUIPMENT RARITY TIERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- [COMMON]    (Slate): Entry-level gear dropped in early worlds (W1-W3) or bought
                       cheaply in the Mystic Shop. (Cost: 25 - 45 Coins)
- [RARE]      (Sky):   Substantial stat upgrades with secondary bonuses.
                       (Cost: 80 - 110 Coins)
- [EPIC]      (Purple): Potent mid-to-late game equipment with multi-attribute
                       amplification and energy regen. (Cost: 210 - 250 Coins)
- [LEGENDARY] (Gold):  High-tier relics and armament dropped by late-game bosses
                       and elite encounters. (Cost: 500 - 580 Coins)
- [MYTHIC]    (Rose):  End-game godlike artifacts featuring monumental combat
                       stats and unmatched energy replenishment. (Cost: 1100 - 1400 Coins)

4. OPTIMAL 5-ITEM LOADOUT RECOMMENDATIONS PER HERO ARCHETYPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. MELEE TANK ARCHETYPE (Shieldmon, Krakenmon, Butchermon):
   - Focus on maximum Damage Reduction, massive Health pool, and sustain.
   - Recommended 5-Slot Build:
     1. Celestial Dragonplate / Aegis of the Ancients (Armor)
     2. Heart of the Sun Ring / Ring of the Primordial (Accessory)
     3. Cosmic Infinity Core / Eye of the Void (Relic)
     4. Excalibur Holy Sword / World Slayer Scythe (Weapon)
     5. Boots of the Wind God (Boots)
   - Total Net Gains: +80 to +180 HP, +20 to +38 DEF, +25 to +40 ATK.

B. MELEE ASSASSIN ARCHETYPE (Assassinmon, Reapermon, Jumpmon):
   - Focus on Burst ATK, Mobility (Speed/Jump), and rapid Energy Regen.
   - Recommended 5-Slot Build:
     1. World Slayer Scythe / Excalibur (Weapon)
     2. Chrono Striders / Hermes Wingboots (Boots)
     3. Heart of the Sun Ring / Void Ring (Accessory)
     4. Cosmic Infinity Core / Phoenix Feather (Relic)
     5. Dragonscale Vest / Shadow Stalker Vest (Armor)
   - Total Net Gains: +40 to +60 ATK, +2.5 to +4.5 SPD, +2.0 to +3.5 JUMP, +1.0 NRG Regen.

C. RANGED SNIPER ARCHETYPE (Archermon, Mikomon, Azuremon):
   - Focus on Extended Range, High Attack Power, and Spacing Mobility.
   - Recommended 5-Slot Build:
     1. World Slayer Scythe / Thunderstrike Staff (Weapon)
     2. Eye of the Void / Cosmic Infinity Core (Relic)
     3. Chrono Striders / Boots of the Wind God (Boots)
     4. Heart of the Sun Ring / Void Ring (Accessory)
     5. Dragonscale Vest / Celestial Dragonplate (Armor)
   - Total Net Gains: +4 to +6 RNG, +35 to +55 ATK, +100+ HP, +2.0 SPD.

D. SPELLCASTER / CASTER ARCHETYPE (Magemon, Enigmon, Shadowmon, Lunarmon, Bombamon, Thundermon):
   - Focus on Energy Regen rate (frequent Ultimates), Spell ATK, and Defensive Shielding.
   - Recommended 5-Slot Build:
     1. Thunderstrike Staff / World Slayer Scythe (Weapon)
     2. Cosmic Infinity Core / Eye of the Void (Relic)
     3. Heart of the Sun Ring / Ring of the Primordial (Accessory)
     4. Celestial Dragonplate / Aegis of the Ancients (Armor)
     5. Chrono Striders / Hermes Wingboots (Boots)
   - Total Net Gains: +1.5 to +2.3 Energy Regen / sec, +45 ATK, +20 DEF, +120 HP.

5. IN-GAME DROP BALANCING & MULTIPLIERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Normal Minions: 8% base drop chance (rolls Common/Rare items).
- Elite Minions:  22% base drop chance (rolls Common/Rare/Epic items).
- Stage Minibosses (Blockman, Living Pyramid, etc.): 55% drop chance (guarantees Rare or Epic).
- World Bosses (King Slime, Frost Wyvern, Dragon King, Giant Wisp, etc.):
  100% Guaranteed drop of 1-2 Epic, Legendary, or Mythic items.
- Reward Multiplier scaling across Worlds (1.0x in World 1 up to 5.0x in World 13)
  also increases coin drops and potion/stone rewards alongside equipment drops.

6. MYSTIC SHOP BALANCING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Consumables:
  * Healing Potion: 15 Coins (Restores 15 HP)
  * Upgrade Stone:  50 Coins (+0.1 permanent stat synthesis)
- Equipment Armory:
  * Common Gear:    25 - 45 Coins
  * Rare Gear:      80 - 110 Coins
  * Epic Gear:      210 - 250 Coins
  * Legendary Gear: 500 - 580 Coins
  * Mythic Gear:    1100 - 1400 Coins
- Selling / Discard: Inventory items can be retained across play sessions or equipped
  to any companion without degradation or durability loss.

G. DIRECT BUYING & AUTO-FORGING:
   * Players can forge or buy crafted equipment directly in 1 click!
   * If base ingredients are already owned in the bag, the game automatically uses them
     and discounts the price by only purchasing the missing ingredients + recipe fee.
   * If no base ingredients are owned, it purchases all base ingredients + recipe fee
     and immediately synthesizes the crafted item.
